LARKSPUR OUTDOOR CO. SHIPPING, RETURNS AND WARRANTY POLICY

Shipping
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs 4.90 euros. It is free for orders above 75 euros. Express delivery takes one working day, costs 12.50 euros, and is available only for orders placed before 13:00. We deliver to all European Union countries, Norway and Switzerland. Customs charges for Norway and Switzerland are paid by the customer.

Returns
Unused products may be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. The customer pays the return postage unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item. Refunds reach the original payment method within 7 working days after we receive the parcel. For hygiene reasons, opened water purifier cartridges and worn base layers cannot be returned. Gift cards are not refundable.

Warranty
Tents and backpacks carry a five-year warranty. Water purifiers carry a two-year warranty, and cartridges are excluded because they wear out with use. The warranty covers faults in material and workmanship. It does not cover normal wear, accidents, or damage caused by freezing. To make a claim, send photographs and the order number to the repairs team.

Repairs
Outside the warranty, we repair tents and backpacks for a flat fee of 35 euros, including return postage. Most repairs are finished within 15 working days. We do not repair cartridges or inflatable mats.

Business orders
Orders of 20 or more identical items receive a 12 percent discount. Invoices are payable within 30 days. Products with printed logos cannot be returned.
