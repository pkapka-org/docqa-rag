# Larkspur Rill Water Purifier Manual

The Rill is a hand-pumped water purifier for hikers. It weighs 410 grams and fits a 63 mm wide bottle mouth.

## Specifications

- Flow rate: 1.4 litres per minute with a new cartridge.
- Cartridge life: 1,500 litres of clear water, or about 600 litres of cloudy water.
- Pore size: 0.1 micron.
- Operating temperature: 1 to 45 degrees Celsius.

## What it removes

The Rill removes bacteria, protozoa and microplastics. It does not remove viruses, dissolved salt or heavy metals. Do not use it with sea water.

## First use

1. Rinse the bottle and the intake hose with clean water.
2. Pump one litre through the cartridge and throw that water away.
3. Check the seal for drips before drinking.

## Cleaning and storage

Backflush the cartridge after every 50 litres by pumping clean water through it in reverse. If the flow drops below 0.5 litres per minute even after backflushing, replace the cartridge.

Before storing the Rill for more than two weeks, soak the cartridge for 10 minutes in a mild bleach solution of 2 ml per litre, then let it dry completely for 48 hours.

### Freezing

A wet cartridge that has frozen must be replaced, because ice cracks the fibres and the damage cannot be seen. In freezing weather, keep the cartridge inside your jacket or sleeping bag.

## Troubleshooting

**The pump handle is stiff.** Grease the O-ring with silicone grease. Never use petroleum jelly, which makes the ring swell.

**Water tastes of plastic.** Pump three litres through the cartridge and discard them.

**Water leaks from the base.** Tighten the base a quarter turn. If it still leaks, order seal kit RS-12.
